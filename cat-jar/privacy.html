Cat Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Short version: Cat Jar saves your game progress on your phone. The current version does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Your best score, fish, discovered cats, rescued cats and settings (sound, vibration) are saved in a file on your device so you can continue where you left off. If Android's backup is enabled on your phone, this file may be included in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you and Google.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version has no accounts, no analytics and no internet access.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for small haptic effects when cats merge. You can turn this off in the game.

Advertising (future versions)

A future version may show optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad to continue"), served by Google AdMob. If that happens, this policy will be updated before release to explain what data AdMob processes (such as an advertising ID), and you will be able to control personalized ads through your device settings.

Children

Cat Jar is a general-audience casual game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its saved data.
